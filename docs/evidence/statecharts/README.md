# Statecharts: decisions

Based on David Khourshid's [Statecharts in 2 minutes](https://x.com/DavidKPiano/status/2116523008323981315). The explainer shows the approved script in [`script.md`](../../../scenes/explainers/statecharts/script.md); each step names the script sentences it shows.

- **Script first.** Two earlier versions were built from a model and a rubric and never said what a statechart is. The script answers the reader's three questions in four paragraphs, and the steps follow it in order. `meta.answer` holds `check` to stating the definition.
- **Concrete before abstract.** The answer-first version opened on the definition over a finished chart, and named "parent" and "regions" before the reader had seen why. Judged by the [rubric](../../EXPLAINER-RUBRIC.md), it was clear but handed its ideas down. It now opens on a plain coffee machine and the change that will hurt it (a power button). The reader predicts the copied arrows, the box arrives as the fix, and the definition is that box's name, in step 3. "Region" is named only after the dashed line has split the box.
- **Two examples, one idea each.** The coffee machine shows a parent state (one POWER_OFF instead of four); the media player shows regions. An earlier version also showed a boolean bug (isPlaying and isPaused both true) as something regions remove, but that came from a redundant pair of flags: the natural ones, isPlaying and isMuted, have no impossible combination, so the claim was misleading and the step is gone. A milk frother added to the coffee machine is the transfer case.
- **Questions the reader answers.** Steps 1, 2, 4, 5, 6 and 8 ask a question in their lines and the next step answers it; the rail hides each answer step's title until it is reached. The three check questions in `meta.checks`, asked after the last step, are about this explainer's own machines (how many transitions, states and combinations), because the first ones, about statecharts in general, were answered just as well by a reader who never saw the explainer.
- **Native drawing from the model.** Every box and arrow is a model state or transition, and parts keep their keys across steps, so the grid's own words move into the regions. A Mermaid state diagram now handles composites and regions ([recipe](../../REFERENCE.md#state-machines-and-graphs)), but it cannot yet add a region by a verified edit, and the frother chart does not fit the stage at a readable size, so the scene stays native. XState 5.33.3 explores every reachable configuration of every drawn chart (`tools/oracle/statecharts.ts`), and the tests check the flattened machines against it, that no reachable configuration puts a region in two states, and that each feature doubles the flat machine but adds two states to the statechart.
- **Against a hand-made page.** In the [explainer benchmark](../../../bench/explainers/README.md), pages the same models wrote without Rayner were preferred in both rounds, because their readers drive a live machine; [#88](https://github.com/adewale/rayner/issues/88) proposes that for Rayner.
- **Limits.** The model covers compound and parallel states with event transitions only. A model reader answered the check questions from the explainer, but a control reader answered them without it, so they do not show what the explainer teaches; no person new to statecharts has tried it. No subject expert has reviewed the scene.

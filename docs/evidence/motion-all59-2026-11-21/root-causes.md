# Root causes of the 59 motion reconstructions

The audit confirms the intended effect, sometimes just one clause, in 37 of 59 reconstructions. Seven require an editorial judgment, six show a different defect, five are stopped before the claimed defect paints, three no longer reproduce it and one exposes a defect in both versions. The six causes below cover all 59 historical repairs. A primary cause groups each entry once. Secondary causes identify compound repairs.

The comparison uses the frozen scenes and corpus at `1522b5040e599edcf9f373ff7e3e3fad9e730bfd`, native footage from the first reviewer, all 13 historical repair diffs, the current mutation edits and the 20 November baseline-subtracted detector receipts. The raw score was 32/59, including seven guard-only rejections. This analysis predates the production repairs. ## Shared causes and controls

### Route, token footprint and docking (26 primary cases)

A centreline or endpoint was chosen independently of the ink it carries and the content occupying that space over time. Two travellers may share a route legally at different times. Reserve a lane using actual label and token extents, separate departure and docking ports, or sequence only the competing flights. Protect the part the reader needs, including non-text station rings and stops. Intentional attachment belongs to a named endpoint/phase.

Keep the fast ordinary covers/occluded-words checks. For narrow confirmed contact or a particular symbol, render the complete supported event and remove the named foreground and victim independently. Compare baseline, mutant and clear control. A broad-phase overlap can prune raster work. It cannot establish contact.

Use the complete password, poll, refusal-key and coverage-list passages. Include clear bystander, clipped/transparent/holed drawing, intended station attachment, two simultaneous disjoint travellers and a stop crossed earlier but clear at landing.

Repair the four evidenced baseline clearance problems and tutorial heading now. Retain current positive detections. Reuse a native clearance assertion for preserved scene contracts. A general-purpose automatic obstacle router or ban on non-text overlap is unnecessary.

### Context lifetime and causal order (11 primary cases)

The model computes the destination immediately. The scene must independently show which context persists, what old output clears, and which prerequisites have appeared before a later action. Fresh and rest can produce the right endpoint with a wrong middle. Keep persistent furniture separate from event-specific results. Use the existing lead/fresh/then, enter and leave at the corresponding beat. Keep each necessary prerequisite visible through its dependent trip.

Add one optional per-event assertion independent of the animation builder. During an exact named transient trip require exact view-scoped keyed subjects present or absent on each moving frame. A state predicate selects the applicable story. Missing/ambiguous trigger and missing required presence fail. Validate disabled/absent subjects from a witnessed endpoint or explicit resolvable subject so a typo cannot pass. Distinguish effective alpha/clip presence from full composited readability.

Check browser rack-before-handout, sessions wax-before-ticket, persistent conversation ledger, and old exhibit or door during the next Poppy trip. Poison order, source survival, broad fresh and missing anchor separately. Permit intentionally withheld answers and a still-visible reference.

Implement a small runtime/check integration for both authored replay and reachable reader events. Carry the event boundary through grouped replay, seek and repeated trips. Leave purely editorial intent with the author who can express a concrete assertion.

### Unique objects and copied information (4 primary cases)

A travelling proxy, its source and its destination use different drawing keys. Generic pass and flight do not know that a live source in a different slot represents the same physical object. Conversely reading a code legitimately copies it. Construct one carry operation that retires the named source as its proxy appears, optionally substitutes a different target drawing on arrival, and lets the caller restore a borrowed source at a later beat. Continue using pass for messages and explicit copies.

Apply the same phase assertion to the source during the named travel. Use a required target during the subsequent action for causal handoff. Judge the moving interval after departure so a co-located two-image crossfade is legal.

A separated wordless solid ring must reject. An outline marking the empty slot, a genuine duplicate value at a separate port and a source-to-target crossfade at one port must pass. Test repeated events, reverse travel and later skill-card restoration.

Use the existing c.travel/leave/enter operations and existing durations. One source of timing avoids separate source leave and mover appear calls drifting. Do not mark every repeated label or equal-shaped item as a duplicate.

### Identity, rearrangement and text replacement (9 primary cases)

The same model identity can keep its key while its screen composition or displayed content changes. Interpolating each matching leaf independently can transport labels, mix incompatible page shapes, or open a temporary gap inside one address. Decide whether a part carries narrative travel, remains continuous context or is replaced at rest. Keep a compound URL and an incompatible page layout atomic. Distinct layout keys and whole-word matching already support replacement.

Where authors have chosen stationary labels, check that their actual readable positions are confined to old/new arrangement or check an invariant relationship between labelled components. Use phase assertions for persistent context. No geometry-only decision can determine whether an unobstructed slide helps the explanation.

Preserve meaningful travel of a person taking a new seat and deliberate camera or layout motion. Separately test an applicable same-key changed word with whole-text and by-place choices. The historical 1,564 identical glyph frames cannot validate those choices.

Keep existing correct reflows. Put narrow endpoint/compound-layout assertions in the owning scene tests for commitments that matter. Do not introduce a catalog-wide displacement threshold.

### Beat accounting and editorial pace (6 primary cases)

Adding serial beats, lengthening fades or stretching the event envelope changed elapsed time independently of the intended teaching sequence. A too-small allowance is mechanical. An acceptable but slow valid sequence needs an author’s timing or narrative criterion. Budget all actual serial and parallel beats for each branch. Collapse redundant legs or shorten a beat only if the explanation remains clear. Preserve the existing refusal to compress an overlong score.

Machine already compares authored duration with allowed runtime on the normal send path. Keep guard-only as a separate outcome. For a specifically agreed time budget or maximum number of legs, test that event using the real clock. The five-second ceiling is a coarse independent regression.

Guard the three historical squeezed edits. Accept a legal fast first direct ask and a complex slow route. For coverage report, assert the two named explanatory legs if that editorial decision is pinned.

No tighter universal time cap. Keep rejection reporting separate from material that actually painted. Add branch-specific time or beat assertions only where an author has established the requirement.

### Legibility of the travel representation (3 primary cases)

A scaled full-detail resting card keeps its printed literal while losing the size needed to read it during motion. Choose a readable travel-sized code when its actual value is meaningful, or carry a wordless symbol and show the literal at rest.

Retain the existing small-words check against whole-picture phone reading size and the scene’s resting words. Confirm scale and real glyph contribution in phone captures.

Reject all three audited tiny-literal examples. Accept a wordless compact token and a readable full-size travelling literal. A large rendering enlarged after capture is an inadequate phone control.

Existing prevention and checker are adequate for these three reconstructions. The independent glyph pairing/provenance problems belong to text identity and inspection.

## Interpretation limits

- The original record consists of repair hunks. They support intent and mechanism, not a frame-exact rerender of the historical revision. The current audit compares the mutations in their frozen contemporary scenes.

- A confirmed verdict can cover one component of a compound historical claim. Unreproduced and secondary-only verdicts are scoped to the documented routes, except the exhaustive two edited glyph steps. The captured 82 pairs comprise 59 primary and 23 supplemental routes.

- Legacy rejection is baseline-subtracted mutation score. Only two 20 November gains required positive attribution. Seven duration-only rejections include two mutations that do paint an effect on other legal routes. A mute diagnostic is not evidence of a clear picture.

- The current geometric rules use approximations. Whole-word coverage can miss a partial edge, the conservative glyph rule needs nearly complete glyph boxes, and shape/shape overlap alone cannot distinguish a docking or road from a forbidden obstruction. The existing aggregate suppresses glyph diagnoses by the traveller if any covers finding names it and keeps one longest covers run per traveller. If reporting multiple victims, deduplicate same victim/mask/time only. These limitations are possible explanations, not per-row instrumented proofs of which conditional fired.

- Presence during travel means the declared subject has visible-effective ink/alpha inside its clip. Full composited legibility and the sign of a tiny grazing contact require a separate renderer-based oracle. A continuous origin/landing crossfade can be intentional.

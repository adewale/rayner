## Correctness by construction and its limits

Each builder enforces the guarantee it has enough information to own:

- A new drawable enters the actual target parent and sibling slot, with its effective target clip.
- A translated carrier gets a shortest orthogonal route that reserves its complete footprint against declared protected subjects, or construction refuses. Meaningful source/destination ports remain chapter decisions.
- An exclusive transfer uses the existing `carry` ownership operation; an information copy uses `pass`. Persistent context is not retired merely because an event has fresh output.
- Tools share one admitted state/presentation inventory, real reader roots, explicit completeness and legal histories. Immediate distinct responses survive deeper review sampling.
- A result receives corpus credit only after process, schema, identity, inventory and target admission. A film generation becomes authoritative only after its complete images exist.
- Native route observation snapshots immutable SVG, restores temporary scene paint before awaiting the native task, and finishes observation before the scene advances. A type-resolved keeper catches missing awaits. Installed-consumer validation also exposed a private renderer dependency in this shared helper; its foreground proof now uses public family order and native SVG composition over black and white paper, without a new public export. The final helper/runtime/await set passes 17 controls, including both aperture colors and an inherited clip.

Independent native pixels, external model evidence, browser behavior and deliberate invalid inputs remain necessary. Reusing the implementation's formula as its expected behavior would not establish the promised consequence.

The router protects only supplied subjects and a fixed translated footprint. It conservatively blocks clipped/invisible obstacles, overreserves an omitted join, does not optimize bends or pedagogy, and does not prove moving/rotating obstacles safe. The Browser review exposed a 17.43-unit collision-free detour; an explicit meaningful read port reduced it to **2.14 units**, the endpoint Manhattan distance, while retaining full-size code and every protected label. [Route reasoning and limits](captures/chapter-adversarial.md).

Generic motion and text rules remain conservative at ambiguous curves, partial glyph clips, unsupported paints, overlapping changing foreground and multi-mask unions. Native evidence samples finite frames and resolutions. Served review samples deeper histories; bounded exploration reports incompleteness. Types and constructors do not establish that the model, prose, causal declarations, ports or teaching choices express the right intent. Accessibility and browser layout retain real-browser owners. There is no new universal detector for unnecessary movement, all narrative causality, all glyph dissolution or all pacing.

The original frozen audit's 37 confirmed, seven editorial, six secondary, five blocked, three unreproduced and one inconclusive verdicts are unchanged. A full new visual re-audit of all 59 variants has **not** been performed. The original 18 generic misses remain qualified by that review; the new two uncredited rows above have explicit dispositions. Twenty uncredited mutations are not twenty broken current scenes, and this work does not justify saying “all possible visual defects are fixed”.

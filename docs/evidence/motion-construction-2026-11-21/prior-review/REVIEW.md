The full CI suite passed **2,157 tests** with 182 explicit skips; embeds passed 223 with one skip and lettering passed 76. Package/release, catalog/budgets and active compatibility jobs passed. All three story shards and their final receipt-merge job also passed. Every active required CI job is green on this head. ## A smaller implementation sequence

1. Repair hierarchy-aware admission and family fades first, with small public fixtures that check native frames and endpoint paint order. This addresses shared causes rather than compensating with more scene routes.
2. Establish one authoritative rendering/visibility boundary. Match the reader's culling and clipping semantics, track particular glyph occurrences, and conservatively confirm ambiguous candidates. Retain the independent scene pixel controls.
3. Expose one repository-internal bounded enumeration of model/presentation origins and edges. Reuse it in check, film and review, and in evidence drivers that derive real reader roots. Keep durable-state and presentation counts separate.
4. Make each scene's lifetime/ownership policy explicit, using existing `pass` versus `carry` and narrow fresh-output roles. Protect unchanged context and every required trip occurrence. An editorial duration policy belongs in an independent chapter owner observing actual intervals, rather than duplicated production arithmetic.
5. Make scoring fail closed on worker/process/inventory errors and exact expected outcomes. Freeze the target observation before the next detector change. Keep historical figures and separate metric categories.
6. Correct source receipt dependencies and make film publication atomic. Then run the focused adversarial fixtures, restored historical controls, and normal CI again.

Keep the useful work: actual-footprint routes, the source-copy/ownership distinction, transient-result reset semantics, conservative fill/clip proofs, the genuine Ring and Periodic-groups repairs, and asynchronous Resvg with its byte-parity evidence. A wholesale rewrite is unnecessary. PR structure can be made easier to review by first resolving the PR 112/main parent integration and then reviewing the motion delta. A direct rebase onto main alone would bring the unmerged parent and frozen benchmark into the diff. Of the declared 609 changed paths, 201 match main byte-for-byte and 284 are retained evidence; 124 are the remaining implementation/test/documentation changes.

## Remaining limits

Authored context declarations identify semantic prerequisites; they do not infer intent from geometry. Their presence test isolates subject ink, while separate native/occlusion checks protect composited readability. Native trajectory checks sample the real scene clock and supported histories; they do not establish every subframe crossing or arbitrary input. Model exploration retains its declared bounds.

The generic travelling-area rule remains an approximation for curves and complex fill overlap. Occlusion attributes the strongest qualifying representative per sampled frame rather than computing the joint causal contribution of arbitrarily many masks. Editorial pacing and uncontracted narrative relationships remain scene-owned. The obsolete SignIn collection-duration example does not acquire an invented universal pacing criterion.

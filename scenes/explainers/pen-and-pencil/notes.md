# Pen and pencil notes

The two simulations separate route construction from ink motion. The chained route turns moves by up to 2° and changes their lengths by up to 3%. The tracing pen uses a damped spring and filtered tremor; these are chosen models, not measured properties of a real hand.

Only seeds 1967 and 7 are pictured. The construction is shared between them, while the decorative marks depend on the seed. The close-up compares actual ink deviation with the construction band; it should not suggest that the drawn ink is mathematically identical across seeds.

The example is one four-period timeline with one event per period. Layout comes from Agentic Mermaid with Inter measurements. The visual influence is credited to pele-art-diagrams. rough.js is a comparison drawn from its documented rectangle-and-seed API, not output rendered in this scene.

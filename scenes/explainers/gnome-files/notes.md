# Construction and scope

The scene uses the report and article linked in its metadata, including the recorded task locations and findings. The opening menu paths are schematic, not reproduced screenshots. The selected participant determines which unfinished task is introduced, while the outcome grid keeps all six participants visible.

Green through black are the report's categories. Assigning them scores from 5 to 1 is this page's visualization choice; the spacing is not a measured interval of difficulty or time. Gray is excluded from the plotted journey rather than averaged in as a success or failure. Ranking by give-ups counts the black cells and is distinct from any broader ranking in the article.

The evidence concerns the two cited Files 47 development builds and six first-time GNOME users. It supports an account of those sessions, not a population estimate or a causal experiment isolating menus from other design factors.

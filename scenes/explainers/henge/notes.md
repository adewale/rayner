# Construction and scope

The street setting changes the bearing from Manhattan's 299.1° to due west. The observer stays at 40.75° N and the sky stays unchanged. The globe view keeps one axis direction; the next view projects the same observer's afternoon paths onto bearing and height above the local horizon. The annual curve then keeps that horizon crossing and varies the Sun's declination. Street-level disc views add the vertical information a top-down bearing cannot supply. The overhead view draws a schematic grid turned to the street's bearing, not Manhattan's real blocks; its sunrise direction is the same day's path reached at the opposite hour angle, and the winter mornings when the eastern end faces sunrise come from the same crossing calculation as the sunset alignments.

The model uses a fixed 23.44° tilt, a circular orbit and spherical horizon coordinates. Longitude zero is the March equinox; the seasonal spacing is conceptual rather than a prediction of calendar dates. The annual sweep times the Sun's centre at a flat geometric horizon. Half/full comparisons use that same bearing at centre heights zero and one solar radius (0.2666°). The day gap between them converts orbital longitude at the mean rate, 365.2422 days per 360°; the real orbit moves more slowly in early July, so the gap is approximate. Refraction, terrain, observer elevation, local obstructions and the Sun's changing apparent size are omitted. The street view enlarges the disc and simplifies the buildings. Actual AMNH event times require a specified visible horizon and an ephemeris; this page does not supply visiting times.

The native coordinate projection is checked against 48 vectors captured independently with ERFA's `hd2ae`, using pyerfa 2.0.1.5. `tools/oracle/henge.py` regenerates those vectors without importing Rayner's model. USNO's altitude/azimuth formula supplies the independent spherical identity; equinoxes, solstice extremes, constant sky under street rotation and the ordering of disc-height crossings are also checked.

Sources: [AMNH event definitions and May/July order](https://www.amnh.org/research/hayden-planetarium/manhattanhenge), [USNO coordinate formula](https://aa.usno.navy.mil/faq/alt_az), [ERFA 2.0.1 horizon conversion](https://github.com/liberfa/erfa/blob/v2.0.1/src/hd2ae.c), [NASA/JPL seasons explanation](https://www.nesdis.noaa.gov/about/k-12-education/understanding-our-planet/why-does-earth-have-seasons), and [NASA's due-west/equinox counterfactual](https://science.nasa.gov/image-article/apod-2014-july-6-manhattanhenge-a-new-york-city-sunset/).

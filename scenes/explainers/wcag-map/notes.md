# Construction and scope

The data is tied to the WCAG Recommendations, dated updates and pinned source documents linked in scene metadata. All three levels and four guideline selections remain available. The opening is an illustration of Focus Visible, not a claim that its outline alone establishes compliance with every focus-related criterion.

Totals are cumulative by level and exclude removed criteria in the selected version. The guideline details retain removed criteria as history. Draft changes are not final requirements: Focus Visible's proposed move to A was reversed before the final 2.2 text. The script summarizes the published criteria; it is not an automated conformance audit or a rule about which version an organization must adopt.

The opener (focus, levels, versions) states every total and the answer. The detail steps that follow use the mind map for what the opener's strip cannot show, which guidelines each version changed, rather than deriving the totals again; a closing step of total cards was removed for repeating the opener. The version strip carries its own colour key, so the colours read with the caption hidden.

2.4.7 Focus Visible's draft history is left off the page: drafts of WCAG 2.2 from WD-WCAG22-20200227 to CRD-WCAG22-20230125 listed it at level A, and from CRD-WCAG22-20230517 on it is AA again, as in 2.0 and 2.1. The change logs of those drafts and of REC-WCAG22-20231005 kept "2019-11-10: Promoted Focus Visible from Level AA to Level A."; REC-WCAG22-20241212 drops that entry. The draft links stay in the scene's sources because the third check question's answer mentions the drafts.

# GTFS notes

The demonstration uses Google’s invented Demo Transit Authority sample feed and the pinned GTFS reference linked by the scene. The June 2007 dates are part of that fixture, not a current transit timetable. The opening compares the selected date with its preceding day, so the Sunday-to-Monday contrast belongs to the default scenario.

The two stops deliberately expose different results: the airport has fixed departures, while Stagecoach includes frequency-based service. A frequency bar spans the hours vehicles leave the first stop; it is not a drawing of one continuously traveling vehicle.

The model covers these sample calendars, trips, stop times and frequencies. It does not model live delays, cancellations from a realtime feed or a complete journey planner.
